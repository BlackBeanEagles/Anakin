Before you send any of it

  • Say it's experimental. Someone who feels misled after handing over a real complaint is a person this harmed, not a data point.
  • Never ask for a portal password. Not in DMs, not "to make it easier". The boundary is the product.
  • Answer every reply. A stranger trusting you with a stuck pension is doing you a favour.
  • Turn off DRY_RUN before you promise anyone anything — otherwise the letters go to outbox/ and nothing is actually filed.
