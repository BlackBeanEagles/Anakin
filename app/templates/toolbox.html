What the pgportal build actually did. Anakin's documentation prices a build at 25 credits. It charged 200 — two thirds of the free tier. It then reported status: success, created the catalog entry "CPGRAMS PG Portal", and published zero actions. Nothing to call, and no refund, because upstream does not consider that a failure.

Persist kept running, because it was built on the assumption that this might happen: an actionless build is recorded as a failure whatever its status field says, and the site is immediately re-read through the ordinary chain so the fallback is measured rather than assumed.
